1Install the extension

  1. Unzip the download. You get a folder named CourtReserveExtension. Put it somewhere permanent — Documents is a good spot. (Chrome loads it from that folder; if you move or delete the folder, the extension breaks.)
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (toggle, top-right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the CourtReserveExtension folder.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Court Helper.

2First-run setup (automatic)

  1. Open CourtReserve in a tab and log in.
  2. Open your club's Calendar (Month view).
  3. Click the Court Helper toolbar icon. Setup runs itself: it finds your club, loads the calendar's categories, and detects when registration opens. You'll see "Setup complete ✓" — click Continue.
  4. In Settings, tick the categories you play (e.g. 4.0+ Round Robin). That's it — dates whose registration opens that day now appear bold on the calendar; hover one to see exactly which events open.

3Try Premium — free, in test mode

Premium unlocks ⏰ Auto-Register: the extension registers you the moment the window opens. During the beta you can activate it with a test card — nothing is ever charged:

  1. Open the extension → Settings → Premium → Upgrade to Premium.
  2. At checkout use any name/address, your real email (that's where the license key goes), and the test card: 
    Card:  4242 4242 4242 4242
Exp:   06 / 32
CVC:   123
  3. After "payment" you'll land on the success page. Click Open the CourtReserve calendar →: an Activate Premium card appears on the calendar with your license key already filled in. One click on Activate license and Premium is live.
⚠ Auto-Register fires only if: Chrome is running, your computer is awake, you're logged into CourtReserve, and the extension is enabled. Mac users — to wake your Mac every morning at 6:58 AM, run this once in Terminal: 
sudo pmset repeat wakeorpoweron MTWRFSU 06:58:00 # check with: pmset -g sched

What happens when we go live?

The beta runs against the payment provider's test environment. When Court Helper launches on the Chrome Web Store, here's the conversion process — no surprises:

  1. Test licenses stop working. All beta license keys are deactivated on launch day. The extension checks licenses about once a week, so Auto-Register will switch off within days of launch and the popup will show Premium as inactive.
  2. Install the live version. Come back to this page (or the Chrome Web Store listing) and install the live build — it replaces the beta.
  3. Activate with a real subscription — $15/year, cancel anytime. You'll get a new license key and activate it the same one-click way.
  4. Your settings carry over — organization, categories, and registration window are stored locally and survive the switch.

Questions testers ask

Does the test card charge me?
No. Test mode simulates everything — the "payment", the email, the license key — and never touches a real bank account.

How does the 1-year subscription work?
A Premium license is valid while your subscription is active. It renews yearly at $15 unless you cancel; if a renewal fails or you cancel, the extension locks Premium at its next weekly license check. Re-activate any time.

My license key didn't arrive.
Check spam for an email from Dodo Payments. Still stuck? Reply to the beta invite and I'll send your key.

Found a bug?
Reply to the beta invite with what you clicked and what happened — screenshots welcome.
